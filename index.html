Roland Bibaj is a Solothurn-based photographer who moves easily between genres — portraits, cars, and nature — without losing his eye for detail. Years spent perfecting fast-action wildlife shots sharpen every frame: an instinct for timing, patience for the right moment, and technical precision throughout. The result is a portfolio as versatile as it is authentic — real people, real machines, real moments.
